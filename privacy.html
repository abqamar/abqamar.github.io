Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information

  Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how abqamar.dev (the "Website"), the personal portfolio of Muhammad Basit Qamar, collects and uses information. I keep data collection to a minimum: the Website has no user accounts, does not use cookies, and does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

This policy covers this Website only. The mobile apps shown in the Projects section were built for clients and are governed by the privacy policies of the organizations that publish them.

Information I collect

The only personal information I collect is what you choose to send through the contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message — and anything you include in it

Please don't include sensitive information (such as passwords, financial or health details) in your message.

How I use your information

I use the details you submit only to read and reply to your message, for example about a project, job opportunity, or consultation. I do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes, and I won't add you to any mailing list.

Third-party services

The Website relies on a few third-party services to work. They may process limited technical data, such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies:

  • Web3Forms — delivers contact form submissions to my email inbox. Privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the Website and may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Privacy statement
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts used on the Website. Privacy policy
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — serves the icon library used on the Website. Privacy policy

The Website also links to external sites such as the App Store, Google Play, LinkedIn, GitHub, X (Twitter), and Instagram. Once you leave this Website, the other site's privacy policy applies.

The Developer Tools section links to microtools.abqamar.dev, a separate site I run. It shows Google AdSense ads and has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

The Website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

How long I keep your information

Messages you send are kept in my email inbox for as long as needed to respond and keep a record of our conversation. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete any personal information you have sent me. Email me at the address below and I will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

The Website is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, please contact me and I will delete it.

Security

The Website is served over HTTPS and form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect the information you send me.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact me at abqamar.dev@gmail.com.
